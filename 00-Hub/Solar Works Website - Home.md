# Solar Works Website — Home

> [!note] Entry Point
> This is the master Map of Content for the Solar Works Website build. Start here and navigate to any domain MOC or atomic note.

## Project Overview

The **Solar Works Website** is a trust-led, testimonial-first marketing and lead-generation website for Solar Works, a residential and commercial solar installation company. The site's primary job is to build confidence through real customer proof and convert qualified visitors into assessment leads via a short form or an AI-enabled chatbot. All leads are stored in a central Google Sheets database with immediate email notification to the sales team.

See [[Project Summary]] and [[Project Scope]] for full context.
