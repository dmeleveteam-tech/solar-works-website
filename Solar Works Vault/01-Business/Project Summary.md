# Project Summary

The Solar Works website shall establish Solar Works as a trusted, high-quality solar installation partner for residential and selected commercial clients. The website must build confidence through real customer proof, explain the value of a properly designed solar system, and convert qualified visitors into actionable leads.

## Definition of Done

Project completion is defined by the launch of a responsive, premium website where visitors can:

- Watch optimized customer videos.
- Easily submit inquiries via a secure [[Lead Capture Form]] or an [[Integration - AI Lead Chatbot|AI-enabled chatbot]].

All lead details (including UTM parameters and consent) must be reliably saved to a [[Integration - Google Sheets Lead Database|Google Sheets database]], triggering immediate email notifications for the sales team.

## Core Objectives

1. Build confidence through real customer proof (testimonial-first).
2. Explain the value of a properly designed solar system.
3. Convert qualified visitors into actionable leads.
