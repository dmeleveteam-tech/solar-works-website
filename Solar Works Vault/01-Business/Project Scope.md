# Project Scope

Defines what the Phase 1 Solar Works website build includes and explicitly excludes.

## In Scope (Phase 1)

- Responsive marketing website covering all pages in the [[MOC - Pages and Features|sitemap]].
- Testimonial and project [[Global Requirements|content management]] capability.
- [[Lead Capture Form]] connected to the [[Integration - Google Sheets Lead Database|Google Sheets Lead Database]].
- [[Integration - AI Lead Chatbot|AI lead chatbot]] with qualification flow, consent logic, and Google Sheets integration.
- [[Integration - GA4 and Meta Pixel|GA4 and Meta Pixel]] analytics with conversion events.
- Privacy Notice and consent capture.

## Out of Scope (Phase 1)

> [!caution] Explicitly excluded
> - Online checkout / e-commerce payment flow.
> - Automatic final quotations or binding savings promises.
> - Customer portal, monitoring dashboard, or service-ticketing portal.
> - Full CRM replacement; Google Sheets is the initial lead database.
